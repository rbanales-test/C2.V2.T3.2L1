Topic 3.2: Subtractive Synthesis Signal Path

Component: Filter (Low-pass)

The filter removes some of the sound to shape the final tone. A low-pass filter is the usual choice for basses, leads, pads and classic filter sweeps. Adding resonance makes the sweep sound more vocal and animated.

Analogy: sculpting marble. A sculptor starts with more stone than the statue needs, then chisels the extra away. Subtractive synthesis starts with more sound than needed, then filters the extra out.
Core concept: MIDI controls do not replace this path. They move the dials along it. An amplitude envelope shapes the loudness over time. A filter envelope changes the brightness over time.
